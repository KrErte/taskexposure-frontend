How exposed is your job to AI?

This tool analyzes what you actually do at work — not your title, not your years of experience — and identifies which parts of your role AI systems can already perform or are likely to perform within the next 2–5 years.

Takes approximately 20 minutes.
Requires honest, specific input.

You will receive:

  • A task exposure percentage
  • An explanation of where that number comes from
  • Concrete actions that reduce exposure
